# Why this, and how it was scored

Picked 12 October 2026 for the Enterprise DNA rebuild lane.

## The bill

BlackLine publishes no price list; every contract is quoted. ERP Research's buyer contract data puts the average around US$77,000 a year, with a range from roughly US$17,500 to US$340,000, and implementation and services adding US$5,000 to US$50,000 or more. It says cost is driven by the modules bought (reconciliations, task management, transaction matching, intercompany), the number of legal entities and the volume of accounts reconciled, not by user seats. Source: https://erpresearch.com/erp-add-ons/financial-close/blackline/pricing (checked 2026-10-12).

## Scores (1 to 5)

| Measure | Score | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Annual bill | 4 | Around US$77,000 a year on average in buyer data, quoted only |
| Data-shaped | 5 | Accounts, balances, checklist tasks, reconciliations and items, journals, explanations, auditor requests |
| Our buyers pay for it | 4 | NZ and AU finance teams, and the accounting firms and outsourced CFOs who close books for clients |
| Search demand | 4 | "BlackLine alternative" and "BlackLine pricing" are searched; page one is rival vendors' listicles |
| One-session buildable | 5 | Every rule is a view; the rest is lists and sign-offs |
| Import path | 4 | Any ledger exports a trial balance; BlackLine lists export to Excel |

Total 26 of 30.

## Rivals

FloQast, Trintech Cadency and Adra, Numeric and Vena also sell close software. None publishes a price.
